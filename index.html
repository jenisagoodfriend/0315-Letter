For You

HALLO IAN It's been six months of you courting me, and honestly, ang bilis isipin, pero ang dami na nating pinagdaanan in those seven months. Alam mo, I'm very thankful that I met you. Ever since you came into my life, parang naging mas magaan ang mundo. The world didn't necessarily become easier, but somehow, having you made everything feel less heavy. You taught me how to take things lightly, how to stop carrying everything alone, and most importantly, you taught me how to finally validate myself. I'm thankful for you because loving you, and being loved by you, taught me so much about myself. You made me discover parts of me that I didn't even know existed. I might not say it as often as I should, but mahal kita. And I don't think my love for you is something that will ever stay the same, because somehow, every day, I find another reason to love you more than I did yesterday. Jhaiden, things weren't always easy for us. There were moments when everything felt too heavy, when misunderstandings made us question things, when fear got louder than the love we had for each other. But somehow, even through all of that, we stayed. And I think that's when I realized that my view of love had changed. Dati, I used to believe that all men were the same. I used to tell myself na kahit kailan, hinding-hindi ako iiyak dahil sa isang lalaki. Na kapag naging mahirap, aalis ako. Kapag nawala yung peace ko, aalis ako. Kapag nasaktan ako, I would choose myself and leave before anyone could hurt me more. Because growing up, leaving felt like the safest thing to do. I grew up seeing how love could hurt people. I saw how something that was supposed to be beautiful could become the reason someone broke. And because of that, I made so many promises to myself. I promised that I would never love someone enough to lose myself. I promised that I would never stay when things became difficult. Looking back now, ang silly pala ng ibang promises ko. Because then you came into my life. You came into my life and taught me how to stay. And Jhaiden, I never regretted it, kahit konti. Because somehow, you made staying feel worth it. You made loving someone feel worth the risk. You're worth it. You have never failed to show me that you love me. In the smallest things, in the moments I don't even ask for anything, in the times when I don't know how to explain what's wrong—you still try to understand me. You make me feel seen. You make me feel heard. You make me feel like I'm worth the effort, worth the patience, and most importantly, worth loving. You're a great lover, Jhaiden. And I hope you know that I notice everything. I notice the little things you do. I notice the way you stay. I notice how you try. I notice how you make me laugh when I don't feel like smiling. I notice how you make space for my feelings, even when they're difficult to understand. I notice every little effort that you probably think is nothing. To you, maybe they're just small things. But to me, they're pieces of the reason why I fell in love with you. You make me feel like I'm worth your love. And every single day, you find another way to make me happy. Jhaiden, I'm sorry. I know I've made you wait. And I'll be honest, there are days when the guilt gets heavy. Every time someone tells me na matagal na, na ang tagal mo nang naghihintay, I carry those words with me on the way home. Kasi kahit ako, alam ko naman. Oo nga. Matagal na. But despite knowing that, I still couldn't bring myself to say yes just yet. Not because I don't love you. It's because I love you enough to want to do this right. I'm a very traditional woman when it comes to these things. I want my family to know you. I want them to meet you. I want them to know that the person I'm choosing is someone who genuinely loves me. And honestly, you know how difficult that is for me. No matter how easy other people make it sound, it isn't easy for me. Because Jhaiden, ayokong mahalin ka nang patago. Ayokong yung pagmamahal ko sayo ay kailangan kong hinaan just because I'm scared of what other people might say.You don't deserve a love that has to hide. You deserve to be loved loudly and proudly. You deserve a love that doesn't have to whisper your name. And I want you to know that I didn't choose you because I was pressured to. I didn't choose you because you waited long enough. I chose you because after everything, sayo parin ang bagsak ko. I don't want to make promises just because they sound beautiful. I know there are promises I might not be able to keep, and I never want to give you something beautiful in words only to fail you in reality. But there is one thing I can promise you with all the certainty I have. I'll love you more each day. And when the universe isn't gentle with us, I hope we remember to be gentle with each other. Because ikaw at ako ang laman ng prayers ko, wishes ko, at sa 11:11 ko. And that's funny, because I was never really someone who wished for things. I never really believed that wishing could change anything. But ever since you came into my life, I started wishing again. I started believing in little things. And sometimes, when it's 11:11, I don't ask the universe for anything grand. I ask for them to give us enough courage to keep choosing each other. And if life is kind enough, I hope it lets me keep you. Because out of all the people I could have met, somehow, I met you. And out of all the hearts I could have loved, somehow, I fell in love with yours. So if one day you ever wonder whether you were worth the wait—you were. If you ever wonder whether I loved you while I was making you wait—I did. And if you ever wonder why I took so long—it's because I wanted the day I finally said yes to mean something. I wanted it to be more than just a word. I wanted it to be a choice. A choice I could make with my whole heart. And after everything we've been through, after every fear, every doubt, every waiting, every little moment that brought us here—I would still choose you. Ngayon, Bukas, at sa susunod pa. And in all the ordinary days in between. Mahal kita. More than I knew I could. And somehow, I know I'll love you even more tomorrow.
